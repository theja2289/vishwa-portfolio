Wafer Guardian · what I do at work, as a game

Match the probe card, catch the bad dies

A small game about wafer test. Match the probe card to the chip, load the wafer, run the prober and catch the bad dies. Then build a MEMS probe card yourself: design the spring, grow it with lithography, let the robots assemble it, measure every tip.

  • Start with Match: read the chip's pad pitch and current, pick the card.
  • Run the prober and tap the dies that fail.
  • Open Workshop to design and assemble your own card.

The work transfers to any process where something small must be handled accurately, identified reliably and proven afterwards.

Wafer Guardian · what we do at FormFactor, as a game

Match the probe card, catch the bad dies

Made so anyone can understand wafer test in five minutes. Match the probe card to the chip, load the wafer, run the prober and catch the bad dies. Then build a MEMS probe card yourself: design the spring, grow it with lithography, let the robots assemble it, measure every tip.

  • Start with Match: read the chip's pad pitch and current, pick the card.
  • Run the prober and tap the dies that fail.
  • Open Workshop to design and assemble your own card.
